### Changed

- **The Forever quest list is gone.** ForeverCore 1.14.5 turned it on for Adventure Guide players; it is removed now, so your quests show in the game's own quest tracker again, or in your quest addon's. The guide's section stays in the Forever column, which sits to the left of the game's tracker. Drag a heading to move it, and right-click one for Lock and Reset position. Nothing to change in your settings. (cjber/forever-core#794)
