### Fixed

- **"Loading quest data..." clears sooner after an update.** The quest catalogue the first login builds takes about 15 seconds at 60 fps instead of 23, and at a low frame rate each frame does proportionally more of the work, so the build no longer slows down with the frame rate. (#839)
- **A late Questie no longer leaves an uncorrected quest list in place.** When Questie reported ready while the first read was still running, that read could finish last and replace the corrected list until the next login.
