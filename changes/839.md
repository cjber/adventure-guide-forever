### Fixed

- **"Loading quest data..." no longer drags on at a low frame rate.** The quest catalogue the first login builds takes about the same time at 20 fps as at 60, around half a minute with Questie, because a slow frame does proportionally more of the work, and no frame of the build stalls the game. (#839)
- **A late Questie no longer leaves an uncorrected quest list in place.** When Questie reported ready while the first read was still running, that read could finish last and replace the corrected list until the next login.
