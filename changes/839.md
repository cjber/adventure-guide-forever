### Fixed

- **"Loading quest data..." no longer takes longer at a low frame rate.** The quest catalogue built at the first login after an update finishes in about the same time at 20 fps as at 75 fps, about half a minute with Questie, because a slow frame now does proportionally more of the work. (#839)
- **A late Questie no longer leaves an uncorrected quest list in place.** When Questie reported ready while the first read was still running, that read could finish last and replace the corrected list until the next login.
