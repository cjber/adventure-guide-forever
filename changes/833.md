### Removed

- **Alt-G opens the guide without a minimap button.** The minimap button, its settings checkbox, the Forever addons page and the install and enable hints for other Forever addons are gone. Alt-G and the key binding still open the guide.

### Changed

- **The tracker settings follow the new tracker.** The "Attach to quest tracker" setting is gone, because the column is pinned beside the game's tracker. "Lock the tracker" stays.
