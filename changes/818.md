### Added

- **Forever quest list, now an Adventure Guide option.** Turn it on in Options > AddOns > Adventure Guide Forever or from the tracker's heading menu: your tracked quests, ready to turn in first, then this zone, then the rest by zone, nearest first. It is off by default, and stays on if you had it on in ForeverCore. While Questie's, Kaliel's or another tracker shows your quests, the option names it and stays off.
