# Guide navigation

The world-map sidebar is the guide. Continue shows two choices and other sections show one each.
Click a section header to expand its remaining choices. Quests in your log includes accepted quests
from the current story and is the default suggestion until the player selects a journey.

Choosing a journey uses the existing choice and guidance preferences. Advice stays separate from
navigation. Combat and Wanderer mode disable travel; unknown destinations remain advice.
The map guide shows active steps first, then the zone quest outline. Future prerequisites never
become navigation targets until they are available.

The tracker uses the journey title, current action and up to two upcoming steps. Live counts agree
with the map and Shortest Path. Hearthstone and teleport instructions use the travel provider's
visible action prompt. Story milestones come only from a proven final chain hand-in, recorded once
per character and shown through the completion popup. Collections remain in Legacy Forever.

The map tab, addon compartment and `/agf` open the same guide. There is no standalone window or
Shift-J binding. Opening the guide leaves a collapsed Blizzard quest sidebar unchanged.
